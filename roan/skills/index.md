# roan/skills/

The skill documents that ship with Roan. `home.ensure_home()` leaves this
directory alone — it is the repo, not the home — and `style.py` copies the one
below into `~/.Roan/skills/` and keeps it current with the upstream. Your own
skills live in the home and are never touched by that.

* [Roan's writing style](roan-writing-style/SKILL.md) - the document the agent applies to everything it writes, with `always: true`.
